## 1. Executive Briefing: The Psychology & Reality Check

### The Student's Dilemma
A Year 10 student who appears detached or says "I don't really care" is almost always dealing with **protective avoidance**. Deep down, he knows GCSEs matter and that Sixth Form is around the corner, but he feels confused, unguided, and overwhelmed. Acting indifferent protects his self-esteem: *if you don't try, you can't officially fail.*

### The Antidote: De-Escalation & Clarity
The cure is not a 50-page lecture or telling him to study 4 hours a night. The cure is **radical simplicity**, **quick wins**, and **demystifying the destination**:

1. **The SFH6 A-Level Reality Check:**
   - To study 3 A-Levels at **SFH6** (Forest Hill & Sydenham Sixth Form), the general entry requirement is an **average GCSE point score of ~5.7**, including Grade 4/5 in English and Maths.
   - For subjects like **A-Level Business, History, or Social Sciences**, they typically ask for a **Grade 5 or 6** in GCSE English/History.
   - **The Translation:** He does *not* need straight 8s or 9s. Solid 5s and 6s with a couple of 7s fully unlocks his dream Sixth Form and puts him on track for top UK universities or high-level apprenticeships. He is on Day 1 of Year 10—he has over 600 days. He is in prime position.

2. **The 20-Minute Daily Rule (Low Activation Barrier):**
   - In Year 10, consistency trumps marathon sessions. 
   - **Rule:** Four 25-minute focused blocks per week (Monday to Thursday) beats 5 hours of stressful, guilt-ridden cramming on Sunday night.
   - 25 minutes of high-yield practice puts a Year 10 in the top 10% of his cohort immediately.

3. **Banning the "Pretty Notes" Trap:**
   - Research (and r/GCSE consensus) proves that re-reading textbooks, writing aesthetic summaries, and highlighting notes produces high exhaustion with near-zero retention.
   - We replace note-taking with **The 3-Step Exam Loop**:
     $$\text{Learn (5-min video)} \longrightarrow \text{Active Recall (Flashcard / Blurt)} \longrightarrow \text{Apply (3 Exam Questions)}$$

## 2. The Core Subjects: Blueprints & "Cheat Codes"

### 2.1 GCSE Mathematics (Edexcel — `1MA1`)

*Exam Board at Forest Hill: Edexcel Higher/Foundation*

#### The Core Strategy
Maths is a physical sport, not a reading subject. You do not revise maths by looking at worked examples; you revise by getting your hand moving with a pen.

#### Top Tier Resources
| Platform | Cost | Best Used For |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **MathsGenie** | **Free** | **The Gold Standard.** Organised neatly by grade (Grade 1 to 9). Every topic has a 10-min video, a PDF question booklet, and full handwritten mark schemes. |
| **The GCSE Maths Tutor** (YouTube) | **Free** | The clearest, calmest video teacher on YouTube for higher-tier concepts (algebraic fractions, circle theorems, functions). |
| **CorbettMaths (5-a-day)** | **Free** | 5 daily questions (Foundation, Foundation Plus, Higher, Higher Plus). Takes 7 minutes a day and stops topics from going rusty. |
| **1st Class Maths** | **Free** | Best known for exam-period predicted papers and hyper-accurate Edexcel topic checklists. |
| **DrFrostMaths / OnMaths** | **Free** | Interactive online practice with automatic marking if he prefers typing on a laptop. |
| **Sparx Maths** | School Paid | Usually set by Forest Hill for homework. Treat Sparx XP as baseline; use MathsGenie for actual exam preparation. |

#### The "Grade 6/7 Engine" Method
1. **The RAG Error Log:** Whenever he does a worksheet or test, mark mistakes in one of two categories:
   - **Fluency / Silly slip:** Misread sign, arithmetic error (fix with 2 minutes of calm checking).
   - **Concept Gap:** Didn't know the first step (go straight to MathsGenie video on that exact topic).
2. **Master the "Guaranteed Questions":** Edexcel tests certain topics on every single paper (indices, factorising quadratics, standard form, ratio sharing, trigonometry). Securing these locks in a Grade 6 before touching hard Grade 8/9 questions.

### 2.2 AQA Combined Science Trilogy (`8464` — Double Award)

*Exam Board at Forest Hill: AQA (2 GCSEs across Biology, Chemistry, Physics)*

#### The Core Strategy
Science has the highest content volume, which makes anxious students freeze. The secret to AQA Science is that the mark schemes are robotic: examiners look for specific **keywords**, not poetic explanations.

#### Top Tier Resources
| Platform | Cost | Best Used For |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cognito** (`cognitoedu.org`) | **Free** (Freemium) | **The #1 Student Favourite.** Short 3-minute animated videos mapped precisely to AQA, followed immediately by 5 interactive recall questions. Tracks percentage completion visually. |
| **FreeScienceLessons** (Shaun Donnelly on YouTube) | **Free** | **The Holy Grail.** Zero fluff, monotone clarity, exact AQA specification wording. If Shaun says a sentence, write that exact sentence in the exam. |
| **Physics & Maths Tutor (PMT)** | **Free** | The biggest repository of topic-by-topic past paper questions and mark schemes. |
| **Save My Exams** | Freemium | Concise, beautifully illustrated revision notes and topic questions. |
| **Seneca Learning** | **Free** | Gamified bite-sized revision; great for low-energy days when feeling unmotivated. |
| **Tassomai** | Paid / School | Daily multiple-choice quizzing app that builds automated spaced repetition. |

#### The Two AQA Science "Hacks"
1. **The Required Practicals Goldmine (15% of all marks):**
   - Across the 6 science papers, **at least 15% of all marks** are based directly on the 21 Required Practicals (e.g., Osmosis, Microscopy, Resistance of a Wire, Chromatography).
   - *Hack:* Learn the Independent, Dependent, and Control variables for all 21 practicals. Shaun Donnelly has a dedicated playlist for every single one. That's up to a full grade jump for free.
2. **Flashcards for Units & Formulas:**
   - In Physics, memorising the formula sheet triangles and SI units ($J, W, N, m/s^2$) guarantees 10–15 raw calculation marks per paper.

### 2.3 AQA English Language (`8700`) & Literature (`8702`)

*Exam Board at Forest Hill: AQA*

#### The Core Strategy
English feels subjective and intimidating because students think "good writing" is an innate talent. It isn't: GCSE English is a formulaic code.

#### Top Tier Resources
| Platform | Cost | Best Used For |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Mr Salles Teaches English** (YouTube) | **Free** | The undisputed master of Grade 7–9 analysis. Teaches you how to write essays examiners actually enjoy reading, rather than standard boring paragraphs. |
| **Mr Bruff** (YouTube) | **Free** | Structured, question-by-question walkthroughs of both Paper 1 and Paper 2. |
| **First Rate Tutors** (YouTube) | **Free** | Clear essay frameworks, quote breakdowns, and exemplar Grade 9 responses. |
| **Genius.com** | **Free** | Best site for crowd-sourced annotations on poetry anthology poems and literature plays. |
| **York Notes / CGP Text Guides** | Paid (~£6) | Reliable character and theme breakdowns for Macbeth, An Inspector Calls, A Christmas Carol / Jekyll & Hyde. |

#### The Formulaic Hacks
1. **Language: The "Question 5 First" Protocol:**
   - On both Paper 1 (creative writing) and Paper 2 (viewpoint article/speech), **Question 5 is worth 40 marks out of 80**—literally 50% of the entire exam.
   - Most students spend 1 hour on Questions 1–4, get tired, and rush Q5 in 25 minutes, throwing away 20 marks.
   - *Hack:* Spend 5 minutes planning Q5, write Q5 first while energy is at 100% (45 mins max), then comfortably finish Q1–4 with remaining time.
2. **Language Q5 Descriptive Grid:**
   - Slicing an image into quadrants (foreground, background, extreme zoom, sensory sound/smell) ensures a dynamic narrative rather than a boring "and then I saw..." list.
3. **Literature: The "Macro-Quote" Method:**
   - Don't try to memorise 50 quotes per book. Memorise **3 to 4 versatile "Swiss Army Knife" quotes** per main character/theme that work across multiple questions.
   - *Example (Macbeth):* *"Stars, hide your fires; let not light see my black and deep desires."* Works for Macbeth, Lady Macbeth, Guilt, Ambition, Deception, Supernatural, and Kingship!

## 3. The Option Subjects: Playbooks

### 3.1 Edexcel History

#### The Core Strategy
Edexcel History is an intense writing speed test. Knowledge is only 50%; the other 50% is strict time management and structure.

#### Top Resources
- **TimelinesTV / History with Mr P** (YouTube): Engaging documentary-style overviews of key topics (Crime & Punishment, Weimar & Nazi Germany, Cold War, Elizabeth I).
- **BBC Bitesize & Seneca**: For fast timeline chronology and key date recall.
- **Edexcel Past Papers & Examiner Reports**: Read the examiner comments to see why students fail high-tariff questions.

#### The Edexcel History Formula
*   **The "1 Mark = 1 Minute" Rule:** If a question is worth 4 marks, spend 4–5 minutes on it. If it is a 12-marker, spend 12–14 minutes. If it is a 16-marker (+4 SPaG), spend 20 minutes. Never run over.
*   **12-Marker ("Explain why..."):** Exactly 3 PEEL paragraphs (Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link). No intro or conclusion needed—it tests explanation, not evaluation.
*   **16-Marker ("How far do you agree?"):** 
    - Intro: Define terms + state a clear thesis/judgment immediately.
    - 2 paragraphs supporting the statement.
    - 2 paragraphs providing counter-factors.
    - Conclusion: Justified final judgment that directly weighs *which factor was the primary catalyst*.

### 3.2 Religious Studies / RE (Philosophy, Ethics & Religion)

#### The Core Strategy
RE is one of the easiest subjects to turn into an 8 or 9 because the question structure is identical across every single topic.

#### Top Resources
- **Ben Wardle** (YouTube): Masterclass videos breaking down themes and 12-mark evaluation questions.
- **Seneca Learning**: Superb for memorising religious teachings and contrasting viewpoints.

#### The 12-Marker Evaluation Engine
Every 12-mark essay should follow the **4-Block Debate**:
1. *Point 1 (Agree):* "Some Christians/Muslims would agree with this statement because... For example, in scripture it states..."
2. *Critique 1:* "However, this argument may be considered weak because in modern society..."
3. *Point 2 (Disagree):* "On the other hand, other believers would argue that... This is supported by..."
4. *Conclusion:* "Overall, the more persuasive argument is [X] because [give definitive philosophical reason]."

*Memorise 6–8 universal scripture quotes* (e.g., Sanctity of Life, Golden Rule, Stewardship) that can be adapted to almost any ethical topic (Abortion, Euthanasia, War, Crime, Environment).

### 3.3 Geography (AQA / Edexcel)

#### Top Resources
- **Planet Lacey / Mrs Geography** (YouTube): Super clear breakdown of physical processes (coasts, rivers, tectonic hazards) and human geography.
- **Coolgeography.co.uk**: Free case-study notes with exact statistics.

#### The Method
- **The Case Study Fact Sheet:** High-tariff Geography questions require named case studies with real statistics (e.g., Typhoon Haiyan, Rio de Janeiro favelas). Create a one-page fact sheet per case study: 3 Primary Effects, 3 Secondary Effects, 3 Immediate Responses, 3 Long-Term Responses.

### 3.4 Edexcel Business Studies (`1BS0`)

#### Top Resources
- **TakingTheBiz** (YouTube): The absolute best Edexcel GCSE Business channel. Entertaining, sharp, directly follows the Edexcel specification.
- **Two Teachers** (YouTube): Animated bite-sized theory explanations.
- **Tutor2u**: Excellent practice worksheets and calculation practice.

#### The BLT Chain of Analysis
For 6, 9, and 12-mark questions, examiners award marks for building deep analytical chains. Never stop at one sentence. Use **BLT**:
- **B**ecause... *(State the direct impact on the business)*
- **L**eading to... *(What happens next? Sales, costs, employee motivation?)*
- **T**herefore... *(The ultimate consequence on profit, cash flow, or market share)*

*Calculation Master:* Practice the 5 core formulas until they are muscle memory: Break-Even point, Net Profit Margin, Gross Profit Margin, Percentage Change, Cash Flow balance.

### 3.5 OCR Sport Science / GCSE PE

#### Top Resources
- **The PE Classroom** (YouTube & Web): Clear breakdowns of anatomy, physiology, and training principles.
- **Planet PE**: Video walk-throughs of key exam topics.

#### The Method
- **The AO1 $\rightarrow$ AO2 Bridge:** OCR exams penalise students who just state textbook definitions (AO1). Every point must be explicitly anchored to a sporting example (AO2):
  - *Weak:* "Continuous training improves aerobic endurance."
  - *Top Mark:* "Continuous training improves aerobic endurance (AO1), allowing a central midfielder in football to maintain high work rates across all 90 minutes without fatiguing (AO2)."

### 3.6 Modern Foreign Languages: Spanish / French (AQA)

#### Top Resources
- **LanguageNut / Quizlet**: Vocabulary sets sorted by theme.
- **Senor Jordan (Spanish) / Learn French with Alexa**: Clear, painless grammar mechanics.

#### The 3-Tense Rule
To jump from a Grade 4/5 to a Grade 7+ in writing and speaking, you must demonstrate **3 core tenses** in every single extended piece:
1. **Present:** (What I normally do)
2. **Preterite / Passé Composé:** (What I did last weekend / on holiday)
3. **Near Future:** (What I am going to do next year)

Add **3 high-level connectives** (*sin embargo* / *cependant*, *por un lado... por otro lado* / *d'un côté... de l'autre côté*, *en mi opinión* / *à mon avis*) to instantly impress the examiner.

## 5. The Mentor 1-on-1 Delivery Guide (For David)

### The Objective
Sit down with him for 15–20 minutes. Your job is **not** to sound like a teacher, parent, or probation officer. Your job is to be the older guy who has walked the path, knows the shortcuts, and is giving him the keys so he stops stressing.

### The 4-Stage Conversation Script

#### Step 1: Normalize & Disarm (Bypassing the "I Don't Care" Guard)
> *"Yo, I know I said I'd catch up with you sooner after that first session, but my own mocks kicked off at the time and got hectic, so my bad on the delay.*  
> *Look, I remember how it felt sitting in Year 10. Teachers start talking about GCSEs like your whole life depends on it, everyone starts throwing exam boards at you, and honestly, the easiest thing to do is just shrug, act like you don't care, and zone out.*  
> *Am I right, or was that just me?"*  
*(Let him speak. Agree with whatever frustrations he shares).*

#### Step 2: The SFH6 Reality Check (Killing the Failure Dread)
> *"Here’s the thing they don't explain to you properly: you do NOT need to be some freak genius who studies 6 hours a day.*  
> *You told me you wanted to do 3 A-Levels at SFH6, right? Business, History, that lane?*  
> *Do you know what SFH6 actually asks for? An average GCSE score around a 5.7. That means getting solid 5s and 6s. That's a strong B-grade. It is completely reachable from right where you’re sitting on Day 1 of Year 10.*  
> *You don't need to stress. You just need to stop doing the stuff that wastes your time."*

#### Step 3: Giving Him the Cheat Codes (The 20-Minute Protocol)
> *"I put together this complete compendium for you. Don't look at it like homework—look at it like the cheat codes to beat the game.*  
> *You know how people spend 2 hours rewriting notes with colourful highlighters and then fail the test? We're not doing that.*  
> *For Science: you watch one 3-minute video on Cognito or FreeScienceLessons. Shaun Donnelly is the goat—he literally speaks the mark scheme.*  
> *For Maths: you do CorbettMaths 5-a-day or MathsGenie. 5 questions a day. 15 minutes. Done.*  
> *Four days a week, 25 minutes after school. That's it. If you do 25 minutes a day, you can play games, chill with your boys, do whatever you want, and you'll still walk into Year 11 ahead of 90% of your year group."*

#### Step 4: Next Steps & Setting Up the Rhythm
> *"Take this doc. Have a scroll through the subjects you're doing. Pick ONE topic this week that you find annoying, watch the 5-minute video, do 3 questions, and see how easy it feels.*  
> *If you get stuck on anything, or you want me to look over an essay or a maths problem, just let me know. I've got your back on this."*
