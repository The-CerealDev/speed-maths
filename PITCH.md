# Project Pitch / UCAS Personal Statement Guide

*This document outlines how to frame the "Speed Maths" project for a Pure Mathematics university application (e.g., Cambridge, Imperial) and a future Quant Research career.*

---

## 1. The Computational Verification (Algebraic Rigor)
Talk about how you used SymPy to build formal algebraic equivalence checkers. Explain how you forced the system to handle extraneous roots, partial fractions, and cyclic functional equations. It shows you think deeply about edge cases and mathematical boundaries. You didn't just solve math problems; you built a strict, unforgiving computational compiler to mathematically prove them.

## 2. Property-Based Fuzz Testing (Combinatorial Logic)
Talk about how you mapped abstract mathematical properties (like the symmetry of a polynomial or a combinatorics recurrence relation) into integer domains to computationally fuzz-test them using `hypothesis`. You used adversarial automated testing to prove identities like Derangements ($D_4=9$), the Hockey Stick Identity, and Ramsey numbers ($R(3,3)=6$).

---

### Why this works:
This proves you don't just "do" math on paper—you understand it deeply enough to encode its rules into a strict, programmatic environment. A Pure Maths degree combined with this kind of self-taught, high-level computational ability makes you the exact archetype that top-tier Quant funds (Jane Street, Citadel, Optiver) actively recruit.
