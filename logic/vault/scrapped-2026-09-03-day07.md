## Scrapped Questions & Original Answers

### A6
- **Original**: Name the fallacy: ``If it is raining, the ground is wet. The ground is wet. Therefore it is raining.''
- **Original Ans**: Affirming the consequent (or the converse error).

### A7
- **Original**: What is the converse of ``If $p$ then $q$''?
- **Original Ans**: ``If $q$ then $p$''.

### A8
- **Original**: To prove directly that ``if $n$ is a multiple of $7$, then $n^2$ is a multiple of $49$'', what is the very first line of the proof?
- **Original Ans**: ``Suppose $n$ is a multiple of $7$, so $n=7k$ for some integer $k$.''

### A10
- **Original**: Which proof technique is most natural for disproving ``every multiple of $3$ is odd''?
- **Original Ans**: A counterexample (e.g.\ $n=6$).
