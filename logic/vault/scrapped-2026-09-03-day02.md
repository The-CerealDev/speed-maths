# Scrapped Questions: Logic Drill #2 (2026-09-03)

## Rationale for Removal
The original Section A and Section B were dominated by elementary flashcards repeating textbook definitions of converse, contrapositive, inverse, and trivial condition checks (e.g. "Is x=3 sufficient for x^2=9?", "Is a=b necessary and sufficient for a^2=b^2?", "True or false: the converse of a true statement is always true").
In TMUA Paper 2, Arg2 (Necessary and Sufficient conditions) is the largest tested category (41 hits in 18 papers). TMUA tests condition strength on actual mathematical structures: roots of quadratics in intervals, polynomial symmetry, calculus monotonicity, and modular arithmetic. These flashcards were replaced with authentic TMUA Arg2 problems.

## Scrapped Questions & Original Answers

### A1
- **Original**: State the converse of: ``If $n$ is a multiple of $6$, then $n$ is a multiple of $3$.''
- **Original Ans**: ``If $n$ is a multiple of $3$, then $n$ is a multiple of $6$.''
- **Replaced By**: For real numbers $x, y$, classify ``$x > y$'' as a condition for ``$x^3 > y^3$''.

### A2
- **Original**: State the contrapositive of: ``If $n$ is a multiple of $6$, then $n$ is a multiple of $3$.''
- **Original Ans**: ``If $n$ is not a multiple of $3$, then $n$ is not a multiple of $6$.''
- **Replaced By**: For non-zero real numbers $x, y$, classify ``$x > y$'' as a condition for ``$\frac{1}{x} < \frac{1}{y}$''.

### A3
- **Original**: True or false: ``$x=3$'' is sufficient for ``$x^2=9$''.
- **Original Ans**: True.
- **Replaced By**: For the quadratic $x^2 - 2kx + 1 = 0$, classify ``$k > 1$'' as a condition for ``the equation has two distinct positive real roots''.

### A4
- **Original**: True or false: ``$x=3$'' is necessary for ``$x^2=9$''.
- **Original Ans**: False.
- **Replaced By**: For real numbers $x, y$, classify ``$|x + y| = |x| + |y|$'' as a condition for ``$xy \ge 0$''.

### A5
- **Original**: Is ``$n$ is a multiple of $4$'' necessary, sufficient, or neither, for ``$n$ is a multiple of $2$''?
- **Original Ans**: Sufficient.
- **Replaced By**: For differentiable $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, classify ``$f'(x) > 0$ for all $x$'' as a condition for ``$f$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$''.

### A6
- **Original**: State the inverse of: ``If $n$ is prime, then $n$ is odd or $n=2$.''
- **Original Ans**: ``If $n$ is not prime, then $n$ is even and $n\neq2$.''
- **Replaced By**: For a $2 \times 2$ system $ax+by=0, cx+dy=0$, classify ``$ad - bc \neq 0$'' as a condition for ``the system has only the trivial solution $(0,0)$''.

### A7
- **Original**: True or false: ``The contrapositive of a true statement is always true.''
- **Original Ans**: True.
- **Replaced By**: For $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2$, classify ``$x^2 + y^2 \le 1$'' as a condition for ``$|x| + |y| \le \sqrt{2}$''.

### A8
- **Original**: True or false: ``The converse of a true statement is always true.''
- **Original Ans**: False.
- **Replaced By**: For a positive integer $n$, classify ``$12 \mid n$'' as a condition for ``$4 \mid n$ and $6 \mid n$''.

### A9
- **Original**: Negate: ``If $n$ is even, then $n^2$ is even.''
- **Original Ans**: ``$n$ is even and $n^2$ is odd.''
- **Replaced By**: For a triangle with sides $a, b, c$, classify ``$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$'' as a condition for ``the area of the triangle is $\frac{1}{2}ab$''.

### A10
- **Original**: True or false: ``$n$ is a multiple of $12$'' is sufficient for ``$n$ is a multiple of $4$''.
- **Original Ans**: True.
- **Replaced By**: For an integer $n$, classify ``$n$ is odd'' as a condition for ``$n^2 \equiv 1 \pmod 8$''.

### B1
- **Original**: State both converse and contrapositive of ``If $x>2$, then $x^2>4$.'' Determine truth of each.
- **Original Ans**: Converse: false. Contrapositive: true.
- **Replaced By**: Necessary and sufficient condition on $k$ for $x^2 - kx + 9 = 0$ to have two distinct real roots strictly greater than 1 (TMUA 2021 P2 Q9 archetype).

### B2
- **Original**: Classify ``$n=6$'' as necessary, sufficient, both, or neither, for ``$n$ is a multiple of $3$''.
- **Original Ans**: Sufficient but not necessary.
- **Replaced By**: Differentiable $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, relationship between $f(-x)=f(x)$ and $f'(-x)=-f'(x)$ (TMUA 2018 P2 Q10 archetype).

### B3
- **Original**: Classify ``$n$ is divisible by $6$'' as necessary, sufficient, both, or neither, for ``$n$ is divisible by $2$ and by $3$''.
- **Original Ans**: Necessary and sufficient.
- **Replaced By**: Positive reals $x, y$, condition $\ln(x+y) < \ln x + \ln y$ vs $x > 1 \land y > 1$.

### B5
- **Original**: True or false: ``If $P\Rightarrow Q$ and $Q\Rightarrow P$ are both true, then $P$ and $Q$ are necessary and sufficient for each other.''
- **Original Ans**: True.
- **Replaced By**: Two triangles $T_1, T_2$ with equal perimeters and equal areas: necessary/sufficient for congruence.

### B6
- **Original**: Given that ``$x$ is rational'' is sufficient for ``$x^2$ is rational'', state the converse. Is the converse true?
- **Original Ans**: Converse: false.
- **Replaced By**: Integer $n$, classify ``$n^3 - n$ is a multiple of 24'' as condition for ``$n$ is odd''.

### B7
- **Original**: State the inverse and converse of ``If $n$ is prime, then $n$ is odd or $n=2$.''
- **Original Ans**: Logically equivalent to each other.
- **Replaced By**: Transitivity failure: $P \implies Q$ and $R \implies Q$ does not imply $P \implies R$ or $R \implies P$.

### B8
- **Original**: True or false: ``$a=b$'' is necessary and sufficient for ``$a^2=b^2$''.
- **Original Ans**: False.
- **Replaced By**: Real $c$, condition for quartic $x^4 + x^2 + c = 0$ to have at least two distinct real roots.

### B9
- **Original**: Is ``$x^2>4$'' necessary, sufficient, both, or neither, for ``$x>2$''?
- **Original Ans**: Neither necessary nor sufficient.
- **Replaced By**: Converse of ``If $9 \mid n \implies 27 \mid n^2$'': evaluate and prove.

### B10
- **Original**: State converse of ``If $n$ is a multiple of $9$, then $n$ is a multiple of $3$.'' Is it true?
- **Original Ans**: Converse: false.
- **Replaced By**: Twice differentiable $f$, classify $f'(a)=0 \land f''(a)>0$ for local minimum at $x=a$.

### C2
- **Original**: Given $P \iff Q$ and $Q \iff R$, prove $P \iff R$.
- **Original Ans**: Transitivity proof.
- **Replaced By**: Cubic $x^3 - 3px + q = 0$ having 3 distinct real roots: necessary and sufficient conditions on $p, q$.

### C5
- **Original**: Test $S$'s converse via its inverse on $8 \mid n \implies 2 \mid n$.
- **Original Ans**: Inverse false, converse false.
- **Replaced By**: Jensen convexity condition for polynomials (TMUA 2018 P2 Q18 archetype).

### C7
- **Original**: $S$ true and $S$'s inverse true, conclude converse.
- **Original Ans**: Converse is true.
- **Replaced By**: Condition for $\exists c \in (a, b) : p'(c) = 0$ for cubics (TMUA 2021 P2 Q8 archetype).
